# PEACE CHARTER OF ORCHIDEA

ORCHIDEA – a symbol of beauty – exists so that people can freely create, learn and understand one another. Beauty
and truth cannot be built with weapons. The authors, maintainers and contributors of ORCHIDEA (including LÝRA,
MYKORÍZA and its other parts) therefore commit to:

1. **Peace.** ORCHIDEA is a peaceful project serving creativity, education, science, culture, memory and mutual
   understanding.
2. **No weapons.** We do not develop features, modules or models intended for war, weapons, military targeting or
   combat control.
3. **No surveillance of people.** We do not develop tools for mass surveillance, for identifying people without
   their knowledge, or for suppressing free expression. Face recognition in ORCHIDEA serves only the person for
   their own library, runs on their device and never leaves it.
4. **Truth and trust.** We do not develop tools meant to deceive. We provide tools of trust: provenance (C2PA) and
   labelling of AI-generated parts.
5. **Clean sources.** We accept no funding, data, models, code or cooperation whose purpose is war, weapons or the
   surveillance of people. For every source we also examine its purpose.
6. **Privacy and freedom.** No telemetry, no mandatory account, no hidden features. People's data belongs to them.
7. **For everyone.** ORCHIDEA is for everyone – regardless of language, country, wealth or device.
8. **Cooperation, not hostility.** We are nobody's enemy. We cooperate through open standards and contribute to a
   new understanding of multimedia.

**Scope.** This Charter binds the project, its maintainers and everyone who contributes to it. It is not part of
the license and does not restrict the rights granted by the Apache License 2.0.

**Changes.** The Charter may only be changed to **strengthen or clarify** its commitments – never to weaken them.
Every change is publicly visible in the repository history.
